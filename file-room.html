What this web application delivers

01 · Regulated E-Signing

IRS Publication 1345 Compliance

Remote signing requires Knowledge-Based Authentication (KBA), a 3-attempt limit, automatic fallback to wet signature, and a tamper-proof 3-year evidence record.

02 · Security & Custody

FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR §314.4)

Financial institution classification mandates MFA, encryption at rest and in transit, malware scanning on upload, and full audit trail logging.

03 · Human Accountability

Does Not Compute Tax

No automated bots computing tax or generating unauthorized advice. Every calculation and deduction is personally prepared and verified by a licensed human professional.
