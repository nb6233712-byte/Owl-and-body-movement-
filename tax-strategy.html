The strategy

Combine the tax planning with
an asset worth owning.

Rather than accept the bill, we looked for a move that did two jobs at once. It centred on the purchase of a $995,000 short-term rental property — but buying it was only the beginning. What mattered was how it was structured and depreciated.

1

Cost segregation

A cost segregation study was performed. Instead of depreciating substantially all of the building over the traditional 27.5-year residential period, the study identified qualifying components that could be depreciated over shorter recovery periods — approximately $248,352 of accelerated and bonus depreciation.

2

The seven-day test

Rental losses are ordinarily passive, and passive losses cannot offset wages. But the average guest stay was seven days or less, which can take the activity outside the definition of a rental activity for purposes of IRC §469.

3

Material participation

The client self-managed the property and participated in its operations. Where the short-term-rental and material-participation requirements are both satisfied, the losses may be treated as nonpassive — and nonpassive losses can offset W-2 income.

Why both halves were necessary

Generating a large deduction was only half the work. A deduction that cannot reach the income it was meant to offset is worth nothing. Combined with other eligible depreciation and deductions on the property, estimated first-year deductions reached approximately $340,676 — and the §469 analysis is what allowed them to land against a salary.

Fit

Worth being honest about.

This is a significant annual commitment and it is not right for everyone. We would rather tell you that on the first call than in the second year.

It earns its fee when
  • Your income is high enough that timing and structure move real money
  • You own a business, or several, and the entity questions are live
  • Equity compensation, real estate or investments make the year complicated
  • You operate or earn in more than one state
  • A sale, a purchase or a liquidity event is somewhere on the horizon
  • You have been surprised by a tax bill more than once
It does not, yet, when
  • A straightforward return is genuinely all you need — take a filing package

If any of these is you, we will say so and point you at the right thing. It costs us a sale and saves you a year.
